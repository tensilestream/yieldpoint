## Why this exists

Yieldpoint is sold under a commercial licence to organisations above the free tier (see
[COMMERCIAL.md](./COMMERCIAL.md)). To offer your contribution under those terms, the
project needs your permission. Without it, one merged pull request would leave a piece of
the codebase that cannot legally be sold, and that piece would have to be found and
removed later — which is far worse for you and for us than asking now.

This is the same reason MongoDB, Qt, Elastic and Grafana ask for one.

## How to sign

Add this line to the message of at least one commit in your pull request:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email>
```

That sign-off means you agree to this document and to the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/). No forms, no
account, no separate service.

## If you would rather not

That is completely reasonable, and there is still a lot you can do:

- File a [false positive](./.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/false_positive.yml) or
  [missed detection](./.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/missed_detection.yml). These are the most
  valuable contributions this project receives, and they require no agreement at all.
- Report a bug, or describe a case the rules get wrong.
- Say what made it confusing. Nothing here is signed to tell us we got something wrong.
